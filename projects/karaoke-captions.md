# Karaoke Captions: Turn local video into editable animated captions and MP4, SRT or VTT exports

Browser-based word-by-word video caption editor using on-device Whisper speech recognition.

[Upstream project](https://karaokecaptions.com/) · [Back to collection](../README.md#transcription-and-dictation)
